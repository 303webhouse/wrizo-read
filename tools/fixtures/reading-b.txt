The ledger is the whole piece and you know it, which is why the middle worries me — you leave the book to explain the grandmother, and the book was doing that better by saying nothing. One line per tree per season: I believed that completely, and I believed the March wind, and I did not believe the sentence about reading a letter you were never meant to open, because it told me how to feel about the thing the ledger had already made me feel. Trust the object. It is colder than you are and it is right. Where you found the weather instead of yourself, I put the piece down and looked out my own window for a while. That is the highest thing I can say about a reading, that it sent me to a window.
