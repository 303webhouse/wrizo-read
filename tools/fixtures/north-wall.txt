The climbers who died on the north wall are remembered by a plaque that faces the wrong way, toward the valley they were leaving, not the summit they were owed. I stood there a long time in bad shoes. A mountain does not know it is a mountain. It is we who insist on the meaning, who need the stone to be an argument, who cannot let a high cold place simply be high and cold and indifferent to our small brave nonsense.
