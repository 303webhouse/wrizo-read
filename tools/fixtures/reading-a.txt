I stopped believing at the ferry, and then I was carried back. The lateness is doing too much work early on — you lean on it, and the reader feels the lean. But the man reading the departures board is exact: I have stood beside that man, wanted to hand him the same small mercy, and swallowed it. What I could not place is the light. Twice it goes gold and goes, and I wanted the second going to cost more than the first, to have learned something from it. Cut the philosophy in the last third; the boat is philosophy enough. The last line is the one I will keep. It does the thing the whole piece was reaching for, quietly, without turning to make sure I saw.
