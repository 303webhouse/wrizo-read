Theme Regime
The charter at the door — three laws every reader signs by entering.

The reading-tone law. Hard on the page, soft on the person. Readings serve the work; they never wound the worker.

The flag law. A “reads synthetic” flag convenes the floor’s committee for a human read. No detector is ever judge. A false flag costs standing.

The seeing law. The house sees only what its laws require: provenance stays coarse, the Scriptor’s seal stays sealed, and every look at the linkage is logged. What the house can and cannot know is published, in plain words.

Questions I’m holdingIs the mother a chapter or a weather system?

My grandfather counted trees the way other men counted debts — often, at night, and never out loud. The ledger has a column for everything except the winter of 1968, which appears only as a torn page and a change in the ink.

I have been trying to write that torn page for eleven years. This is the twelfth attempt, and the first one where I let the tear stay.
