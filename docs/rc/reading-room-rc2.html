Room color

A room of one’s own

Every writer keeps a Room in the Reading Room: their hung pieces, their bio in their own hand, their walls set to their own color. Rooms belong to their writers — the house provides the walls; the writer decides what the room feels like.

Readers follow Rooms, not feeds. When a writer you follow hangs something new, it finds you quietly — no counters, no streaks, no noise.

Try the room color — the accent is the writer’s to set.
