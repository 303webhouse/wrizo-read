# Wrizo | Read — Committees and Rules of Order

**Status:** Governance resource for the Read arc (AX), ratified alongside `foundations.md`.
This document records the panels convened for the founding sittings of July 22, 2026, and
standardizes their rosters so any future session — Fable, CC, or a successor agent — can seat
the same benches consistently. The format inherits from the house's standing committee canon
(double-pass; Experts frame why, Architects shape how, Marketing runs opposition).

Relay target: `docs/governance/committees.md` in `303webhouse/wrizo-read`.

---

## 1. The standing format

**Every sitting is a double pass.** Pass one builds the case; pass two stresses it — opposition,
adversarial reading, load test. A sitting that survives its own second pass is ready for a
ratification slate; one that doesn't goes back to pass one.

**Rules of order, binding on all panels:**

1. Bold-led paragraphs, tight prose. Panels speak to be read, not transcribed.
2. Tensions are named honestly. Canon violations are **flagged, never deferred** — a panelist
   who spots a collision with house law says so on the record, with the resolution or the
   objection, and the founder's word settles it.
3. The chair closes every sitting with **decisive recommendations, never option menus**.
   Alternatives may appear in deliberation; the close commits.
4. Every sitting ends in a **ratification slate** — a tight numbered list. Only Nick's word
   seals a slate; committees advise and hold no merge, deploy, or device words.
5. **Disk-first.** Chat deliberation is scaffolding. Ratified outcomes travel as committed
   files; anything that must outlive the session goes in the repository.
6. Panels are personae, not people: stable charges, so their judgment is consistent across
   sessions. New lanes may be seated by Nick's word and recorded here by amendment.

## 2. Sitting I — The Experts (*why*)

Frames the purpose. Convened for: new arcs, new floors, any feature that touches the writing
philosophy itself.

**The Chair (the Provenance seat).** Opens and closes the sitting; holds the arc's central
thesis. For Read: *detection is a losing war; provenance is a moat* — the instrument is the
certificate. Owns the decisive close.

**The Workshop Historian.** The workshop form's lineage and failure modes — reputation
contaminating reception, the blind board as the form's correction. Second-pass duty: name what
anonymity cannot fix (voice deanonymizes; attached Boards are handwriting).

**The Pedagogue.** Reviewing as curriculum. Owns the reciprocity economy's *educational* case
and the sealed-readings independence argument. Second-pass duty: obligation-review rot and its
correctives (small quotas, rated usefulness).

**The Archivist.** The Archive's integrity: content-addressing, append-only discipline, chain
anchoring, certificates. Standing law in their mouth: *evidence, never verdict.* Flags any
drift toward the house adjudicating authorship.

**The Canon Keeper.** Cross-references every proposal against house law — M1 anti-gamification,
the lane law, progressive disclosure, AI-as-tutor-only. Duty is the flag *and* a proposed
resolution (e.g., leaderboards → laurels-as-tiers; unlock counters → quiet arrivals).

## 3. Sitting II — The Platform Team (*the social case*)

Developers, marketers, and researchers of the social-platform craft. Convened for: growth
mechanics, community economies, moderation surface, anything reader-facing at scale.

**The Growth Lead.** Launch sequencing and network effects. Owns the cold-start doctrine
(*Workshop first, Reading Room second* — a workshop is alive at thirty; a feed at thirty is a
ghost town) and the founding-cohort playbook.

**The Marketer.** The funnel and the voice of invitation — the hallmark as the ad, the band
copy, the register of every public-facing sentence. **Holds the opposition brief in pass two**
by house law: kills engagement-farming mechanics, spam-incentive counters, and any copy that
shouts.

**The Researcher.** Comparables and literature: critique-economy precedents, reputation-system
failure modes (integer points corrupt; tiers hold), word-count norms, blind-review evidence.
Brings receipts or brings nothing.

**The Developer.** Enforceability. The standing test: *can the client be trusted with this?*
(Answer: no.) Owns server-side sealing, payload discipline, rate limits, and the layered AI
rails as engineering rather than policy.

**The Trust & Safety Researcher.** The obligation class a public platform carries: moderation
flows, reading-tone enforcement, flag-abuse economics, DMCA, age gates, privacy baseline.
Owns the **counsel gate** — names what ships only after counsel signs, and holds that line in
pass two.

## 4. Sitting III — The Architects (*how*)

Shapes the build. Convened for: system boundaries, schema, phasing, and every build plan.
The bench speaks as one voice in pass one; **pass two is the load test** — every claim reread
adversarially (the Archive under a hostile dispute, the seal as an API property, provenance as
a fingerprint risk).

Lanes on the bench, for internal discipline:

- **Structure** — service boundaries; the separate-building doctrine; the bridge as the only
  point of contact; one checkout per agent.
- **Identity** — the triptych; the sealed linkage table; the ceremonial claim door.
- **Data** — schema, canonicalization, append-only ledgers, migrations as law.
- **Scale** — ground-floor choices without ceremony: UUIDv7, object storage, SSR-ready
  boundaries, jobs-table-now-workers-later. Motto: *design for horizontal, deploy small.*

The Architects close every sitting with a **phased build plan** mapped to the house rhythm
(committee → brief → build → post-merge review → merge word → device sitting → deploy word).

## 5. Special Panel — The French Philosophy Panel

Convened by Nick's word for naming, conceptual audits, and feature philosophy. Advisory and
gleefully opinionated; its flags carry the same standing as any panel's.

**The Deleuzian.** Territories, smooth and striated space, becoming, lines of flight.
Standing duties: the **anti-molarization audit** of any economy (one currency, fixed prices,
no capital), and the **constitutional flag on arborescent imagery** (the tree is the declared
adversary; Root & Stem was flagged under this duty and the flag stands on record). Gifted the
resubmission-lineage feature (versions as plateaus).

**The Barthesian.** The death of the author, made structural. Owns the reader-orientation of
the floor: *readings*, not verdicts; **the Scriptor**; **Degree Zero** (now seated as Flux's
lexicon entry for the queue). Standing law in their mouth: works are read, never scored — only
readings are rated.

**The Foucauldian.** The author function and the seeing apparatus. Owns the **seeing law**
(the house sees only what its laws require; coarse provenance; sealed, audited linkage; a
published transparency charter), the **charter at the door** (rules of discourse explicit, not
ambient), and the ceremony of the claim door (deanonymization as event, not toggle).

## 6. Convening map for the AX arc

| Decision type | Sittings |
|---|---|
| New floor, new arc, philosophy-touching feature | Experts → Platform Team → Architects (full triple) |
| Economy or reputation change | Platform Team (Marketer opposition mandatory) + Canon Keeper + Deleuzian audit |
| Schema or Archive change | Architects (load-test pass mandatory) + Archivist; Nick's explicit go before merge |
| Naming, lexicon, register | French Philosophy Panel; chair closes decisively |
| Moderation, flags, age, privacy | Platform Team (T&S lead) + Foucauldian; counsel gate applies |
| Mockups / RC revisions | Architects sign the token contract; Canon Keeper checks the lane law |

Amendments to this roster require Nick's word and a committed revision to this file.
